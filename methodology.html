Methodology Overview

This page explains how we calculate Helipad Grades and long‑ball environment rankings. Our model incorporates factors such as ballpark dimensions, wind speed and direction, temperature, humidity, roof status and pitcher handedness. We focus on variables that have measurable impact on ball flight and run production.

Each component is weighted based on its empirical influence on batted balls. The resulting grade ranges from A (most hitter friendly) to F (least hitter friendly). Grades are recalculated for each game using forecasted conditions and public data.

Data Sources

Our data comes from publicly available sources, including weather forecasts, ballpark information, MLB game schedules and historical park factors. We also reference research on how temperature and wind affect carry distance.

Model Limitations

These analytics are for informational purposes only and are not intended to be gambling or betting advice. The model does not account for every possible factor (e.g., player‑specific performance, ballpark altitude changes or unreported injuries) and cannot guarantee outcomes. Always consider other variables and use your own judgment.
