About This Site

This site provides daily MLB environment analytics focused on ballpark conditions, weather, wind, roof status, park factors and hitter‑friendly game environments. Our unique "Helipad Grade" synthesises key variables to identify games where hitters are likely to benefit from favourable conditions. We emphasise transparency, evidence and high‑quality content.
